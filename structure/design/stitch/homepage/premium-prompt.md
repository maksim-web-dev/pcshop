## Prompt Text (copy from here)

---

Design a complete, high-fidelity desktop homepage for **PCShop** — an online computer hardware store selling PCs, laptops, monitors, and peripherals. This is the **Premium Minimalism** visual theme.

**Viewport:** 1440px wide desktop. Design the full page from top to bottom, including header, hero, all content sections, and footer. Do not design only the above-the-fold section.

---

### Brand and Product Context

PCShop is a modern e-commerce store for computer hardware. The Premium Minimalism theme targets design-conscious buyers aged 28–45 who expect quality, restraint, and clarity — not promotional noise. The store sells hardware products (laptops, PCs, monitors, keyboards, mice) with detailed technical specifications. Products are photographed on clean white or warm neutral studio backgrounds.

This is **not** a fashion store, a cosmetics brand, or a luxury jewelry store. It is a premium technology retailer with a refined, editorial visual language.

---

### Page Objective

Design the PCShop HomePage with the following sections in order:
1. **Header** (sticky navigation bar)
2. **Hero Banner** (editorial full-width, 520px tall)
3. **Featured Categories** (product type navigation cards)
4. **Top Products** (curated product card grid, 4–6 products)
5. **Footer**

---

### Target Audience

Design-conscious technology buyers. They value quality signals: generous whitespace, editorial typography, uncluttered product presentation. They are comfortable with higher price points and expect the UI to reflect the quality of the products sold.

---

### Complete Page Layout

**Header (72px tall, sticky):**
- Background: warm off-white `#FAFAF8`, 1px bottom border `#E5E0DB`
- Left: PCShop wordmark in Playfair Display — elegant, typographic
- Center: navigation links in DM Sans 14px — "Laptops", "PCs", "Monitors", "Peripherals"
- Right (left to right): search icon, cart icon with count badge, ThemeSwitcher (small text toggle showing "Premium")
- No heavy icons, no colored backgrounds, no promotional strips

**Hero Banner (520px tall):**
- Full-width, no gradient, background `#FAFAF8`
- Left half: headline in Playfair Display 56px (2–3 words, e.g. "Crafted for Excellence"), subheadline in DM Sans 18px describing a product benefit, one rectangular CTA button in `#1A1A1A` with white text ("Shop Now")
- Right half: a large, clean product photograph (laptop or monitor) on a neutral white/cream background — image occupies the full right panel height
- No carousel, no animated elements, no promotional badge overlays

**Featured Categories (section below hero):**
- Section heading in Playfair Display 28px, left-aligned: "Explore by Category"
- 3–4 large editorial category cards in a row
- Each card: large product category image (top), typographic category label below in Playfair Display (e.g. "Laptops", "Monitors", "Peripherals")
- White card background, 4px radius, subtle shadow
- Generous whitespace between cards and around the section

**Top Products Grid:**
- Section heading in Playfair Display 28px: "Featured Products"
- 4–6 product cards in a 3-column grid (or 4 cards in 2 rows of 2)
- Each product card shows:
  - Product image occupying ≥55% of card height, full-width, clean white/neutral studio background
  - Product name in DM Sans 14px 600 weight below the image
  - Price in DM Sans 16px 600 weight
  - 2–3 small parameter badges (e.g. "16 GB RAM", "512 GB SSD") in `#F4F3F0` background, `#5C5C5C` text
  - "Add to Cart" button — visible at rest but elegantly minimal
- Card background: `#FFFFFF`, 4px radius, soft shadow
- Generous whitespace between cards

**Footer (dark):**
- Background: `#1A1A1A`
- 4-column layout: Shop, Account, Information, Contact
- Text in DM Sans 12px, muted warm gray
- Section headings in DM Sans 14px 500, `#FAFAF8`
- No decorative elements, no illustrations — pure typographic footer

---

### Visual Identity

**Color palette:**
- Page background: `#FAFAF8` (warm off-white, like high-quality paper)
- Card/surface background: `#FFFFFF`
- Primary text: `#1A1A1A` (near-black graphite)
- Secondary text: `#5C5C5C`
- Accent/CTA: `#1A1A1A` (same as primary text — no competing accent color)
- Borders: `#E5E0DB` (warm tan)
- Section alt background: `#F4F3F0` (warm light gray)

**Do not add blue, purple, green, gold, or any decorative accent colors.**

---

### Typography Direction

**Two typefaces only:**
- **Playfair Display** (serif): all headlines, page title, section headings, category labels
- **DM Sans** (humanist sans-serif): all body text, navigation links, badges, prices, buttons

**Type scale:**
- Hero headline: Playfair Display 56px, weight 400 — editorial, not bold
- Section headings: Playfair Display 28px, weight 400
- Product names: DM Sans 14px, weight 600
- Price: DM Sans 16px, weight 600
- Navigation links: DM Sans 14px, weight 500, `#5C5C5C`

Letter spacing on headlines: +0.02em (slight openness). No all-caps headlines.

---

### Spacing and Visual Density

**Loose density.** Whitespace is a design element, not wasted space.
- Page gutter left/right: 80px (not 20px or 40px — generous margins are required)
- Between sections: 64px vertical spacing
- Between product cards: 24px gap
- Internal card padding: 16px
- Hero section: 60px vertical padding top and bottom

If the design looks "empty", it is correct. This theme is deliberately spacious.

---

### Hero Banner Composition

**Reference:** The Apple Mac catalog page shows how editorial product photography can anchor a clean white/neutral layout. Adapt the principle — do not reproduce the layout.

Hero should feel editorial, like a high-end technology magazine spread. The product image is the hero. Text is secondary and tasteful. There is no urgency, no countdown, no "limited offer" language.

**Mock content for hero:**
- Headline: "Crafted for Performance"
- Subheadline: "Powerful laptops. Refined design. No compromises."
- CTA button: "Shop Laptops"
- Featured product image: a slim, premium laptop (MacBook Pro style — white/silver device on cream background)

---

### Product Card Design

**Style:** Minimal, editorial. The product image does the talking.

Each card in the Top Products section shows:
- Full-width image at top (55% of card height) on white/neutral background
- Clean white card below image with 16px padding
- Product name: DM Sans 14px 600, `#1A1A1A`, max 2 lines
- Price: DM Sans 16px 600, `#1A1A1A`
- 2 parameter badges in warm gray pill (`#F4F3F0` bg, `#5C5C5C` text, 2px radius)
- "Add to Cart" text button or minimal rectangular button, `#1A1A1A` background, visible at rest

**Mock products to show (use representative names):**
1. Laptop Pro 15" — from $1,299 — badges: "16 GB RAM", "512 GB SSD"
2. UltraBook Air 13" — from $899 — badges: "8 GB RAM", "256 GB SSD"
3. Studio Monitor 27" — from $649 — badges: "4K UHD", "USB-C"
4. Mechanical Keyboard — from $189 — badges: "Cherry MX", "Wireless"

Do not show star ratings, reviews, discount badges, or "Best Seller" labels.

---

### Product Photography Direction

All product images should appear to be photographed on **white or warm cream studio backgrounds**. Products are hardware only — no people, no lifestyle imagery. Products should be well-lit with soft, diffuse lighting — no dramatic shadows or colorful reflections. The image should feel like it belongs in a product catalog from Apple, Leica, or Dyson.

---

### Category Presentation

Featured Categories section shows 3–4 product type navigation cards:
- "Laptops" — image of a laptop on cream background
- "PCs" — image of a desktop PC or tower
- "Monitors" — image of an ultrawide or premium display
- "Peripherals" — image of a keyboard or mouse

Each card has a large image (top 65% of card) and category label below in Playfair Display.
On hover: very subtle image scale `1.02` and shadow lift. Nothing more.

---

### Header and Navigation

The header is a **fixed navigation bar** at the top of the page, 72px tall.

Logo: "PCShop" wordmark in Playfair Display — left-aligned, `#1A1A1A`. Understated and typographic.

Navigation links (centered or left-of-center): Laptops | PCs | Monitors | Peripherals | Sale
Links in DM Sans 14px, `#5C5C5C`. On hover: `#1A1A1A` with 2px underline.

Right side of header:
- Search icon (magnifier, line style)
- Cart icon (bag or cart, line style) with a small count badge in `#1A1A1A`
- ThemeSwitcher: a small, discreet text control showing "Premium ▾" or three small dots/icons

No search bar in the header on desktop (search expands on icon click).
No promotional banner above the header.

---

### ThemeSwitcher Appearance

The ThemeSwitcher is a compact UI control in the top-right of the header.
It shows the current theme name: "Premium" in DM Sans 12px, `#9E9E9E`.
Clicking reveals a small dropdown with the three theme options: Premium | Gaming | Professional.
The control is minimal — no icon, no highlighted background at rest.

---

### Footer Design

A clean, dark typographic footer. No decorative elements.

Background: `#1A1A1A`
4-column desktop layout:
- Column 1 "Shop": Laptops, PCs, Monitors, Keyboards, Mice
- Column 2 "Account": Sign In, My Orders, Wishlist
- Column 3 "Information": About, Delivery, Returns, Privacy Policy
- Column 4 "Contact": Email, Phone, Social media links (icons only)

Section headings: DM Sans 14px 500, `#FAFAF8`
Links: DM Sans 12px, `#9E9E9E`, hover `#FAFAF8`
Bottom bar: copyright line in DM Sans 11px, `#5C5C5C`

---

### Accessibility Requirements

- Minimum text contrast: 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text (≥24px regular)
- All interactive elements must have clearly visible hover and focus states
- CTA buttons must be visible at rest without requiring hover
- No text smaller than 12px (11px for captions only)
- Color is not the only indicator of interactive state

---

### Reference Image Instructions

**REF-P02 (Apple Mac catalog):** Reference for overall catalog page whitespace, product card proportion (image top, text below), and horizontal tab sub-navigation. Adopt the principle of image dominance and clean white card treatment. Do not reproduce Apple's specific layout, branding, or product lineup.

**REF-P05 (Teenage Engineering products):** Reference for editorial confidence and extreme whitespace upper bound. Teenage Engineering uses black backgrounds — PCShop premium uses warm off-white. Take only the principle: product photography as the total focus, no background competition. PCShop's version has warmer colors and must show prices and CTAs clearly.

---

### Design Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- ❌ Dark or colored backgrounds anywhere on the page (except footer)
- ❌ Gradients on buttons or section backgrounds
- ❌ Pill-shaped buttons with high border-radius (use 4px rectangular)
- ❌ Gold, rose-gold, or decorative metallic accents
- ❌ Star ratings, review counts, or social proof widgets on product cards
- ❌ Promotional urgency elements ("Limited Time", "Only 3 Left", countdown timers)
- ❌ Hero carousel or multiple hero slides
- ❌ Heavy icons or illustrated mascots
- ❌ Lifestyle photography with people
- ❌ All-caps headlines (all-caps labels only, never headings)
- ❌ Script or decorative fonts of any kind
- ❌ Blue, purple, green, red, or gold color accents

---

### Expected Visual Deliverable

A single, full-page desktop design at 1440px width showing:
- Complete, designed header (not a placeholder)
- Full 520px hero banner with actual mock content
- Featured categories section with real imagery and labels
- 4–6 product cards with product names, prices, badges
- Footer with 4-column layout and actual link text

The design must feel like a completed, production-ready concept — not a wireframe, not a low-fidelity mockup, not a generic e-commerce template, and not an Apple Store replica.
