## Research Brief

**What we are looking for:**

A product detail page, catalog layout, or hero banner that communicates:
- Dark, immersive environment that keeps focus on the hardware
- Purposeful use of purple and/or cyan accent color — not scattered neon everywhere
- Typography that feels technical and assertive without being unreadable
- Product photography that looks powerful and high-performance
- Glow/lighting effects that add dimension without cluttering the UI
- CTA buttons that are always visible (not hover-only) — gaming users act decisively

**We are NOT looking for:**
- Neon overload — 5+ competing accent colors
- Text that is unreadable due to low contrast against dark background
- Animated backgrounds that distract from the product
- Extreme RGB every-element styling

## Anti-References — What to Avoid

| Anti-pattern | Description | Why problematic |
|--------------|-------------|----------------|
| Neon overload | 4+ accent colors used simultaneously (purple + cyan + green + red + yellow) | Visually chaotic; reduces focus on product |
| Animated hero backgrounds | Full-screen looping particle systems, animated mesh gradients at rest | Distracts from product; performance cost; triggers reduced-motion violations |
| Unreadable decorative typography | Broken, glitch-effect, or extreme condensed type at body sizes | Fails readability requirements; alienates non-gaming audience |
| Cluttered backgrounds | Hexagonal grids, circuit board patterns, overlapping geometric shapes behind content | Creates visual noise that fights the product |
| Excessive product badges | 5+ sticker-style badges per product card ("HOT", "RGB", "TOP PICK", "SALE", "NEW") | Looks low-quality; credibility damage |
| All-caps body text | Using uppercase for body descriptions, not just labels | Reduces reading speed; inappropriate at body text sizes |
| Hover-only CTAs | Add-to-cart requiring hover to appear (see premium review finding) | Inaccessible on touch; also bad UX for decisive gaming buyers who want to act immediately |
