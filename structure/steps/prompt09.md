## 5. Apply project owner review findings

### Shared findings

- All themes must represent the same store.
- Preserve identical product identities, prices and business functionality.
- Product availability must be visible in all themes.
- Use consistent branding and contact information.
- Remove unsupported commercial promises and links to nonexistent features.
- Preserve equivalent search functionality.
- Preserve the theme switcher.
- Preserve accessible primary actions.
- Treat generated product images as mockup assets.
- Identify external image dependencies.

Do not invent real company contact information.

### Premium improvements

Preserve the refined editorial identity, warm neutral palette, serif headings and generous whitespace.

Improve:

- Header readability.
- Search discoverability.
- Product name and specification readability.
- Product availability presentation.
- Navigation clarity.
- Visual balance of the featured product grid.

Do not make the design resemble a generic marketplace.

### Gaming improvements

Preserve the dark atmospheric visual identity, purple and cyan accents, Rajdhani typography and expressive product photography.

Improve:

- Secondary text contrast.
- Footer readability.
- Search and navigation visibility.
- Product availability indicators.
- Product grid balance for six products.

Consider a 3-column featured product grid on the homepage while preserving a denser catalog layout.

Avoid excessive neon and decorative effects.

### Professional improvements

Preserve the blue and graphite color palette, Inter typography and commercial usability.

Improve:

- Hero banner composition.
- Distinctive visual identity.
- Typography hierarchy.
- Category card presentation.
- Product card polish.
- Visual depth and spacing.

Create a sophisticated contemporary electronics storefront rather than a generic template.

Remove the unsupported fast-delivery promise.

Do not introduce additional business features.
